🔒 척척콜 개인정보처리방침

시행일: 2026-09-11

척척콜(이하 "회사")은 『척척콜』(이하 "서비스")을 제공하며, 이용자의 개인정보를 소중히 다룹니다. 척척콜은 계정 없이(로그인 없이) 동작하며, 통화 요약·전사(받아쓰기)는 이용자 기기 안에서 처리되어 서버로 전송되지 않는 것을 원칙으로 합니다.

이 문서는 마지막에 명시된 시행일 기준이며, 변경 시 본 페이지에서 고지합니다.

1. 요약 (한눈에)

  • 척척콜은 회원가입·로그인이 없고, 이름·이메일 같은 계정 정보를 수집하지 않습니다.
  • 통화 녹음 파일, 받아쓰기 전문(transcript), 요약은 기기 안에서만 만들어지고 저장되며, 회사 서버로 업로드하지 않습니다.
  • 문자 내용·전화번호·연락처·통화 내용은 분석/광고 목적으로 외부에 전송하지 않습니다.
  • 앱 개선을 위한 비식별 사용 통계·오류 진단·결제 정보만 외부 처리자(구글, Mixpanel, RevenueCat)를 통해 처리됩니다.

2. 기기에서 접근·처리하는 정보 (기기 내 처리, 외부 전송 없음)

서비스 제공을 위해 아래 정보에 접근하며, 별도 언급이 없는 한 기기 내부에서만 사용·저장됩니다.

  • 사용 통계 및 진단: 기기 종류·OS 버전·앱 버전·통신사·언어 등 기기 정보, 앱 사용 이벤트(예: 화면 표시·기능 사용), 앱 설정·권한 보유 상태(비식별 플래그), 익명 기기 식별자, 오류(크래시) 로그
    • 처리자: Google Firebase(Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), Mixpanel

CheokCheokCall Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-11

CheokCheokCall ("we") provides CheokCheokCall ("the Service") and takes your privacy seriously. CheokCheokCall works without any account or login, and call summaries and transcription are, by design, processed on your device and not sent to our servers.

This document is effective as of the date above. We will post any changes on this page.

1. At a glance

  • CheokCheokCall has no sign-up or login and does not collect account data such as your name or email.
  • Call recordings, transcripts, and summaries are created and stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Message content, phone numbers, contacts, and call content are never sent externally for analytics or advertising.
  • Only de-identified usage statistics, crash diagnostics, and purchase data are processed via third-party processors (Google, Mixpanel, RevenueCat) to improve the app.

2. Information accessed and processed on your device (on-device only, not transmitted)

  • Callback messages are configured and sent by you and delivered to the recipient over the carrier network (we do not collect the message content).

3. Information collected and transmitted automatically (to operate and improve the app)

To improve and operate the Service, the following de-identified information is processed via third-party processors. It does not include message content, phone numbers, contacts, call content, or recordings/transcripts/summaries.

  • Usage statistics & diagnostics: device model, OS version, app version, carrier, language and similar device info; app usage events (e.g., screen views, feature usage); app settings/permission-state flags (de-identified); an anonymous device identifier; crash logs
    • Processors: Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), Mixpanel
  • Push notification token: a device token used to deliver notifications
    • Processor: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Purchase/subscription info: purchase/subscription status, an anonymous billing identifier (App User ID)
    • Processors: RevenueCat, Google Play Billing
  • Model downloads: network requests to download on-device model files (information incidental to the connection, such as IP)

5. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data (call-log cache, transcripts, summaries, memos, recording references): deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data.
  • Processor data: retained for the period defined by each processor's policy, then deleted.

6. Your rights and how to delete data

  • Revoke permissions: Settings > Apps > CheokCheokCall > Permissions (some features may be limited).
  • Delete data: Uninstall the app, or use Settings > Apps > CheokCheokCall > Storage > Clear data.
  • Minimize analytics: You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • To request access to or deletion of information held by processors, contact us below.
